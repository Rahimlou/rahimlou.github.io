Mycology Links

MMC (Mycology and Microbiology Center)

University of Tartu research network focused on mycology, microbiology, microbial ecology, and global biodiversity data resources.

Timothy Y. James Mycology Lab

James' lab studies fungal genetics, evolution, ecology, and systematics, with research spanning fungal biodiversity, mating systems, pathogens, and genome evolution.

Mycology Collections Portal

It is an online fungal biodiversity database that provides access to millions of specimen records, images, distribution maps, and collection data from herbaria, museums, and research institutions worldwide to support mycological research and education.
